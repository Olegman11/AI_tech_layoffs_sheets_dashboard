# AI_tech_layoffs_sheets_dashboard
Tech Layoffs &amp; Hiring Trends 2026
As a novice data analyst, I decided to analyse modern workforce trends in the global tech industry, including redundancies, hiring patterns, AI adoption, employee sentiment and market conditions.
First, I found an interesting dataset on Kaggle about Tech Layoffs and Hiring Trends. I downloaded it and took a sample of 1,000 rows to make it easier to work with.
Then, I started the data cleaning process. I carefully checked for duplicates and empty cells, but it turned out the dataset was already perfectly clean (which was a nice surprise!).
After that, I did some feature engineering. For example, I combined the 'month' and 'year' columns into a proper 'Full Date' column so my line charts would work correctly. Finally, I created several pivot tables to answer key questions (like layoffs by industry and reasons for layoffs) and built the visualisations for my dashboard.

If we look at the top-level KPIs straightaway, you can see the broader picture: across our dataset, we’ve recorded approximately 3.29 million layoffs against 4.88 million open roles. Interestingly, the average job security score sits at a moderate 5.5 out of 10, indicating a climate of uncertainty amongst employees.
Moving down to the left, our Net Workforce Trend chart maps out the timeline. As you can see, the data fluctuates significantly month-to-month, allowing us to identify exactly when workforce contractions hit their peak.
To understand the 'Why' and the 'Where', we can look at the two charts on the right. The doughnut chart breaks down the Reasons for Layoffs. It clearly highlights how factors like 'AI Automation', 'Cost Cutting', and 'Overhiring Correction' are driving these changes. Just below that, the Layoffs by Industry bar chart pinpoints which sectors are being hit the hardest—giving us a clear view of exactly where the market is most volatile.
The true value of this dashboard, however, lies in its interactivity. Using the slicers at the top right, stakeholders can instantly filter all this data by a specific Country or Company Size to drill down into localised, actionable insights.
